Questions Before You Consult?

Quick answers to common questions about the consultation process.

What details should I keep ready?

If a birth-chart consultation is required, keep your date, time and place of birth ready. For general guidance, simply explain your question.

Can I consult through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on the website to start a conversation directly with DurgaAstrologer.

Are consultations private?

We designed the website around direct, private conversations. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

Which problems can I discuss?

You can ask about love, marriage, relationships, family, career, business, Vastu and other personal life questions.

Do you provide instant consultation?

Availability depends on the astrologer and current workload. Call or WhatsApp to check availability.

Is astrology a guaranteed prediction?

Astrology should be treated as guidance or a reflective practice, not as a guarantee of future events or outcomes.
